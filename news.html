Hubble gallery showcases gravitational lenses

A NASA feature presents a gallery of gravitational lenses observed by Hubble. These warped and magnified views help astronomers study distant objects and prepare follow-up science with the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope.

10 November 2026 · NASA

Could gravitational waves reveal new worlds?

Two NASA-led studies describe how the future LISA gravitational-wave observatory could help reveal a population of exoplanets through the effects of orbiting systems on gravitational-wave signals. This is a proposed scientific capability, not a confirmed planet discovery.

2 November 2026 · NASA

Hubble investigates missing oxygen in dwarf galaxies

Observations of 37 nearby dwarf galaxies found less oxygen than researchers expected. The result raises questions about how gas and heavy elements move into and out of small galaxies.

15 September 2026 · Webb

Webb reveals a dynamic star-forming region

New observations of IC 348 show newborn stars shaping their surroundings and identify very low-mass brown dwarfs, including objects with masses around twice Jupiter's. The findings add clues about how stars and planetary-mass objects form.

3 September 2026 · ESO

MOONS instrument achieves first light

The new MOONS spectrograph at ESO's Very Large Telescope made its first observations. It is designed to observe up to 1,000 objects at once, helping researchers study stars and galaxies using visible and infrared light.

11 August 2026 · ESA/Webb

Dust and water observed near the Milky Way's central black hole

Webb observations indicate that dust and water can persist close to Sagittarius A*. The evolved star IRS 3, located about 0.55 light-years away, appears to contribute material despite the extreme environment.

How to read research news

A press release is a summary, not the full scientific paper. Check the publication, methods, sample size, uncertainty, and whether a result is a direct observation, an interpretation, or a future possibility.
